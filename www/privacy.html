Privacy Policy

Macbeth: Urban Reign app. Last updated: [DATE].

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts. The app has no accounts.

Ads

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for a reward, and an occasional full-screen ad between chapters. Google may collect a device advertising identifier, your approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. On iPhone you are asked first whether apps may track you; if you say no, ads are not personalized. See Google's policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying Remove Ads or the King's Bundle turns off the ads between chapters.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm which purchases you own, using an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history; see revenuecat.com/privacy.

Game progress

Your saves and best score stay on your own device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Sharing and tickets

When you tap Share, your phone's own share sheet opens and you choose what to send. Ticket links open the seller's website, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is rated 17+ and is not directed at children under 13.
