Game controls

D-pad moves. A punches (tap three times for a combo), B kicks, FYE (Y) ups your fye; press it again to shoot. CREW gives orders, SWAP changes guns, REIGN is your special. In the car, B brakes and Y lets Banquo shoot.

No sound?

Turn off silent mode and tap SOUND in the game. Sound starts after your first tap.
