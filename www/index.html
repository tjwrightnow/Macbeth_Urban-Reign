The story

The streets are watching

In the gritty streets of Houston, a ruthless hustler with raw talent rises to power after a fateful encounter with three mysterious figures who predict his destiny. His rise comes at a bloody cost as he betrays everyone who ever trusted him.

This modern reimagining turns the Scottish king into an ambitious rapper and dealer determined to dominate by any means necessary. Ambition, betrayal and the brutal price of unchecked power, set against the Houston skyline.

“Every king's reign must end.”

I

The Prophecy

Three figures step out of the shadows and name Macbeth king hereafter. The words wake an ambition he has kept quiet.

II

The Betrayal

Pushed by Lady Macbeth, he kills Duncan, the respected head of the label. The deed marks him and starts the spiral.

III

The Fall

Bodies pile up and enemies close in. Guilt and paranoia eat what is left, until Birnam Wood comes to Dunsinane.

“So foul and fair a day I have not seen.”

Macbeth, Act I

The official game

Play Urban Reign

Live the film as an 8-bit side-scroller. Fight out of Club Plex, outrun HPD on I-45, and take the crown, then hold it when Birnam Wood comes to Dunsinane.

  • Eight stages across Houston: Third Ward, Houston University, the downtown skyline
  • Drive a candy slab on swangas, a Lambo, or a hover bike
  • Find the three witches hidden in every stage
  • Two endings, decided by one choice at the banquet
